Ingredients

  • 6 Tortilla
  • 1 can of Corn
  • 500 grams of chicken
  • 1 can of kidney beans
  • 4 tomatos
  • 6 eggs
  • 350 grams of cottage cheese

  2. Fry the eggs until done
  3. Slightly heat the tortilla until nice
  4. Assemble to your liking
